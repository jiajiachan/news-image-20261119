# Sum up 19 November's world news in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty `news-image-20261119`.

The image sums up Thursday 19 November 2026:

- Top: the historic trilateral summit in Shenzhen — the United States,
  China and Russia flags above three leaders at one table, a white dove
  overhead — after which the three issued a "peace declaration".
- Bottom left: the scales of justice over a courthouse — the UN's top
  court ruled Myanmar committed state-sponsored genocide against the
  Rohingya, a first of its kind.
- Bottom middle: an oil tanker near the Strait of Hormuz with a missile
  streak and Riyadh's skyline — Houthi missiles damaged Riyadh's tallest
  tower and tankers were hit by mines east of Hormuz.
- Bottom right: wind turbines under a globe — COP31 climate talks in
  their final stage.

`main.py` copies `entry.jpg` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte.
No network, no randomness: two runs write identical bytes.
